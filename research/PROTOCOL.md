# Five-year evaluation protocol — frozen before viewing results

Benchmark: Nifty 500 **price index** (`^CRSLDX`) for market regime, industry/theme context and forward portfolio comparison. It is not a hard stock-level qualification gate. Individual stocks qualify on absolute trend and confirmation; simultaneous candidates are ranked by 20-session stock return minus the median eligible return of their own current industry. Stock prices are Yahoo adjusted prices (split/dividend adjusted); the benchmark price index omits dividends. This mismatch favours stock returns and must be visible; report sensitivity to an additional 2% annual benchmark dividend yield. No substitute index may silently take its place.

Evaluate the five calendar years ending on the latest complete common benchmark session, plus at least one warmup year. The current inherited NSE registry is survivorship-biased: missing former listings and historical membership prevent a definitive claim of investable alpha. IPOs enter only when they actually have sufficient trailing history. No historical market-cap column or current theme classification is used to select historical trades.

Entries: next exchange session open. Ignore the signal close as an execution price. Baseline outcomes: next-open to closes at 5, 10, 20, 40 and 60 sessions. Deduplicate an ongoing episode per stock/setup until an intervening close below its original reference. A history gap leaves an observation unresolved; report unresolved counts, never silently treat these as wins. Prices are not forward-filled for signal generation or trade outcomes.

Measure net positive fraction, benchmark-beating fraction, median/mean return, median excess return, 10th/90th percentile outcomes, maximum favourable/adverse excursion after entry, chance of reaching +5/+10%, and same-bar ambiguity for +10% versus -5% first-passage. MFE is an opportunity statistic, not a realised return. Net returns assume 0.25% per side; stress at 0.5% per side. An extra 1% adverse entry-price stress tests late execution.

Chronological split: earliest three years development, next one year validation, last one year holdout. Purge observations whose exit reaches the next partition (60-session maximum horizon); no model choice may use holdout results. Baseline is unchanged. Only three predeclared alternatives: (A) no chase, maximum signal extension 5% and five-day return 10%; (B) Nifty 500 close above its trailing 200-session mean; (C) A+B. These apply to the same baseline detections, never use subsequent returns in selection, and are not an unrestricted parameter search.

Selection objective: 20-session mean net excess after an additional 2% annual benchmark-yield allowance on development and validation. An alternative needs >=100 resolved observations in each partition, positive mean adjusted excess in both, and at least 0.5% validation improvement over baseline. If none qualifies, retain baseline without an improvement claim. Evaluate the selected variant once on holdout. A change to live rules requires credible positive holdout excess and enough observations; survivorship and execution limitations still prevent treating a passing test as proof.

Report yearly and setup-level results, sample sizes, unresolved counts, and 95% intervals from resampling entry calendar months (signals are correlated). Also simulate a finite-capital, no-leverage portfolio: ten equal cash sleeves, one stock per sleeve, one open position per stock, 20-session close exits, maximum two positions per current industry, rank simultaneous entries by detection-day stock return minus eligible industry median, then participation, idle cash earns zero. Industries with fewer than three eligible histories on a date are left unranked and sort behind ranked candidates. Compare full-period CAGR and drawdown with buy-and-hold of the same price index; add dividend-yield sensitivity. Do not describe overlapping signal medians as portfolio performance.

Keep the baseline and all variants visible. Failed or inconclusive tests are publishable results. Do not optimise on the final holdout after reading it.

Data quality: permit at most 1% missing Nifty 500 sessions and list their exact dates. A trade comparison crossing a missing benchmark observation remains unresolved; never fill its return. For the separate buy-and-hold equity curve only, carry the last observed mark on a missing quote day and report the missing-mark count. The initial benchmark purchase and final comparison prices must exist. The original downloaded input artifact is reused when repairing evaluation code; it is not replaced opportunistically based on outcomes.
