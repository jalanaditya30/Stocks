How Stocks works

Confirmed moves

A close above the prior 60-session high, followed by at least one more close holding that same level. The crossing must be within the last five sessions. Price must be above its rising 20-session average and its 50-session average, with positive five-session and 20-session absolute returns. Nifty 500 does not decide whether an individual stock qualifies.

Current technical support

Four visible checks describe the current setup: VStop (10, close, 2) bullish, RafaelZioni OBV MACD (DEMA 9 / EMA 26 / slope 2) bullish, ADX at least 20 with +DI above −DI, and a 20-session Efficiency Ratio of at least 0.25. The audit did not show that 4/4 produced better forward returns than 3/4, so the default order is newest signal—not check count—and no combined win score is shown.

Sector and theme rotation

Industry and curated-theme states use 20D and 60D relative performance against Nifty 500, breadth above moving averages, five-session breadth change, and the share of members above VStop. Candidate tie-breaks compare each stock’s absolute 20-session return with the median eligible member of its own industry. Opening a theme or industry shows every registered member, with current scanner highlights first. Members with at least 65 comparable sessions keep descriptive technical and volume analysis even when they fail strict scanner continuity, freshness or liquidity rules; they are labelled context-only and cannot become signals. Curated themes may overlap and remain broader rotation context; neither theme nor Nifty 500 is a hard stock-entry gate.

Leaders resuming

A prior advance of at least 10%, followed by a ten-session consolidation no wider than 12% with at least three down closes, then a move above the prior 20-session high that holds for at least two closes. A positive absolute 60-session return is also required.

Participation and chase risk

Recent five-session median cash turnover must be at least 1.2× the preceding twenty sessions. The last three closes must average at least 55% of their daily ranges. For context, the tool also shows mean volume over five sessions divided by mean volume over thirty, average 20-session cash turnover as a percentage of estimated live market cap, and the share of 20-session directional volume occurring on up days. These extra readings are sortable diagnostics, not additional entry gates. Both lists require at least ₹2 crore median daily turnover over sixty sessions and nearly continuous trading. Moves more than 8% beyond the breakout, 12% above the 20-session average, 15% over five sessions, or with a daily move above 15% are moved out of the confirmed lists for separate review.

Charts

Individual and All charts views align adjusted candlesticks, traded volume, VStop and the breakout reference on one timeline from 1 January. All charts is available for saved stocks, uploaded custom symbols and every registered member of a selected theme or industry where usable price data exists. Theme outperformers have a positive 20-session return above the group’s eligible-member median; this is an inspection label, not a buy rule. Move or drag across a chart to inspect each completed session’s date, OHLC, daily move and volume. Chart layout changes inspection only; it does not change the signal.

Data and personal workspace

Only completed sessions are used. Adjusted prices measure performance; unadjusted close × volume estimates cash turnover. Volume is traded volume, not delivery volume. Historical theme memberships and registry coverage are not point-in-time. ASM/GSM, circuit restrictions, news and actual execution remain manual checks.

Saved stocks, uploaded symbol lists, review decisions and notes are saved only in this browser. Registered NSE symbols with sufficient context history retain descriptive analysis; strict scanner failures are disclosed and cannot create signals. Export a backup to move devices. Your workspace is not committed to the public repository.
