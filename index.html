How Stocks works

Confirmed moves

A close above the prior 60-session high, followed by at least one more close holding that same level. The crossing must be within the last five sessions. Price must be above its rising 20-session average and its 50-session average, with a positive five-session return and positive 20-session relative performance against Nifty 50.

Leaders resuming

A prior advance of at least 10%, followed by a ten-session consolidation no wider than 12% with at least three down closes, then a move above the prior 20-session high that holds for at least two closes. Positive 60-session relative strength is also required.

Participation and chase risk

Recent five-session median cash turnover must be at least 1.2× the preceding twenty sessions. The last three closes must average at least 55% of their daily ranges. Both lists require at least ₹2 crore median daily turnover over sixty sessions and nearly continuous trading. Moves more than 8% beyond the breakout, 12% above the 20-session average, 15% over five sessions, or with a daily move above 15% are moved out of the confirmed lists for separate review.

Established leaders

The top decile of eligible stocks by return from 252 sessions ago to 21 sessions ago, excluding the latest month. This is descriptive context, with no claim that the different formula tested in Sector-data validates this version.

What a label means

Confirmation is observed price behaviour, not a guarantee of further upside. These initial thresholds are explicit research hypotheses. Theme labels describe breadth and relative performance; they do not gate stock selection. Stocks are ordered by relative strength, then participation, not an opaque score.

Data and personal workspace

Only completed sessions are used. Adjusted prices measure performance; unadjusted close × volume estimates cash turnover. Historical theme memberships and registry coverage are not point-in-time. ASM/GSM, circuit restrictions, news and actual execution remain manual checks.

Watchlists, review decisions and notes are saved only in this browser. Export a backup to move devices. Your notes are not committed to the public repository.
